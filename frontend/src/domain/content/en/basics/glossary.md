**Basis point (bp)** — one hundredth of a percent: 30 bps = 0.30 %.

**Baseline (random)** — the result of entering at random times with the same exits; what the market itself gave. A strategy must beat it. See [How we judge a strategy](/guide/how-we-judge).

**Bot** — a program that runs one strategy with its own fictitious capital, its own risk limits and its own results.

**BTC** — Bitcoin, the reference of the market radar: other coins are measured against it.

**Cooldown** — the waiting time before a bot may trade the same coin again after an exit.

**Drawdown** — the fall of a bot's balance from its highest point. *Max drawdown* is the deepest such fall so far.

**Fill** — the execution of an order, at the price(s) found in the order book.

**Lag / stale data** — data arriving late. A coin whose data is more than a couple of seconds late is marked *stale* and the bots skip it until it is fresh again.

**Momentum** — the tendency of what has been rising to keep rising for a while.

**Net / gross** — a result after costs (*net*) or before costs (*gross*). Everything shown on 0-hl is net unless stated.

**Order book** — the list of buy and sell orders waiting on the exchange, with their prices and amounts.

**Overfitting** — tuning a strategy so closely to past data that it fails on new data.

**Pair** — a coin quoted in another currency, such as SOL/USD.

**Paper trading** — trading with fictitious money against real market prices; orders are simulated, never sent.

**Percentage point (pp)** — the difference between two percentages: 2.4 % − 1.0 % = 1.4 pp.

**Profit factor** — total gains divided by total losses. Above 1, the strategy makes money.

**R** — the amount at risk on a trade (the loss if the stop is hit). Results are often counted in R. See [Stops, targets and R](/guide/stops-targets-r).

**Slippage** — the extra cost when an order has to use worse prices deeper in the order book.

**Spread** — the gap between the best buying price and the best selling price at a given moment.

**Stop-loss** — the price at which a bot sells to limit a loss.

**Study** — a replay of a strategy on recorded market data, judged with criteria fixed in advance.

**Taker / maker** — a *taker* accepts a price already offered (higher fee); a *maker* places an order that waits in the book (lower fee, but not always filled).

**Volatility** — how much a price moves around, measured on its minute-by-minute changes.

**Win rate** — the share of trades that ended with a gain, after fees.
